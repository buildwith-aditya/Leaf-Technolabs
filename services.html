ERPNext services for every stage of growth.

Whether you're evaluating ERPNext for the first time, rescuing a stalled implementation, or scaling an existing system — there's a Leaf Technolabs service built for that moment.

01 — Strategy first

ERP Consulting & Process Mapping

Software can't fix a process nobody understands. Before any implementation, our consultants sit with your teams to map how work actually flows — sales to dispatch, procurement to payment, hiring to payroll. The result is a clear-eyed solution blueprint: which ERPNext modules fit out of the box, what needs customising, what should change in the process itself, and what it will all cost.

02 — The core engagement

ERPNext Implementation

Our flagship service: a structured, milestone-driven rollout of ERPNext across your organisation. We handle company and module setup, chart of accounts, item and warehouse structures, taxation, user roles and permissions, master data migration, UAT cycles and go-live — with a hyper-care window after launch so your team is never alone in week one.

03 — Make it yours

Customisation & Frappe App Development

No two businesses run alike — and the Frappe Framework is built for exactly that. We design custom DocTypes, automated workflows, server and client scripts, branded print formats, dashboards and reports. For deeper needs, we build complete custom Frappe apps that sit cleanly alongside ERPNext, keeping your upgrade path safe.

04 — Safe passage

Migration to ERPNext

Outgrown Tally? Paying too much for SAP, Oracle or Zoho? We plan and execute migrations to ERPNext with discipline: data extraction, cleansing and transformation; trial-balance reconciliation; opening balances; parallel runs where needed; and a hard cut-over only when the numbers tie out. Your history comes with you — verified.

Integrations & APIs

Your ERP shouldn't be an island.
