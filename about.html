Home  /  About Our story

Small team. Open source. Big intentions.

Leaf Technolabs was founded on a simple observation: great ERP shouldn't be reserved for companies with enterprise budgets. ERPNext changed that — and we're here to help businesses make the most of it.

Why "Leaf"?

A leaf is small — and it powers the whole tree.

That's how we think about ERP. The right system, well implemented, quietly powers everything: cash flow, inventory, people, customers. Our name is a promise — we build systems that turn your daily operations into growth.

We're at the beginning of our journey as an ERPNext and Frappe service provider, and we treat that as an advantage. Every client gets our founders' direct attention, fresh implementations on the latest Frappe versions, and a team that has to earn its reputation on your project — because that's exactly what we're doing.

  • Founder-led delivery. The people you meet in the sales call are the people on your project.
  • Open source, all the way down. We contribute back to the Frappe ecosystem we build on.
  • We run on what we sell. Our own CRM, HR, accounting and helpdesk run on the Frappe suite.

Mission & vision

Make world-class ERP a default — not a luxury.

Our mission is to give growing businesses the operational clarity that big enterprises pay millions for — through honest consulting, careful implementation and open-source software. Our vision is to be the partner clients recommend without being asked.

What we stand for

Values we actually practise.

01

Honesty over upsell

If a module won't pay for itself, we'll tell you to skip it. Long-term trust beats short-term invoices, every time.

02

Process before software

We fix the workflow first, then automate it. Automating a broken process just makes mistakes faster.

03

Ownership, end to end

One accountable team from kickoff to support. No hand-offs into the void, no "that's a different department."

04

Teach, don't gatekeep

We document everything and train your team to be self-sufficient. Dependency is not a business model we want.

05

Open by default

Open-source software, open communication, open pricing. You'll always know what we're doing and why.

06

Craft in the details

Clean configurations, readable code, thoughtful print formats. Small details compound into systems people love.

How we work

Partnership, not procurement.

We keep our client list deliberately small so every project gets senior attention. Weekly demos, a shared project tracker (on Frappe, naturally), and honest status updates — even when the news is "we're behind, here's the recovery plan."

  • Weekly working demos — you see progress, not status reports.
  • Shared issue tracker — every request logged, visible and prioritised together.
  • Documentation included — SOPs and admin guides ship with every project.
