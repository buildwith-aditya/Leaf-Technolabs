FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is ERPNext, and why choose it over SAP, Oracle or Zoho?+
ERPNext is a full-featured, 100% open-source ERP built on the Frappe Framework. It covers accounting, inventory, sales, purchase, manufacturing, HR, projects and more — without per-user licence fees, vendor lock-in or rigid customisation limits. You get enterprise-grade capability at a fraction of the total cost of ownership.
How long does an ERPNext implementation take?+
A focused implementation for a small or mid-size business typically takes 6–12 weeks. Larger, multi-entity or heavily customised rollouts can run 3–6 months. After our discovery phase we commit to a milestone-based timeline so you always know what's next.
Can you migrate our data from Tally, Zoho, SAP or Excel?+
Yes. We routinely migrate masters (customers, suppliers, items, chart of accounts) and transactional history from Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, SAP, Odoo and spreadsheets — with validation and reconciliation reports so nothing is lost in translation.
Do you provide support after go-live?+
Always. Every implementation includes a hyper-care period, and we offer flexible AMC plans with defined SLAs covering bug fixes, version upgrades, new reports, user queries and ongoing enhancements.
Cloud or self-hosted — which should we choose?+
Both work beautifully. Frappe Cloud gives you managed hosting, automated backups and one-click upgrades. Self-hosting (on your own servers, AWS, etc.) gives you maximum control and data residency. We'll recommend the right fit based on your compliance needs, team and budget.
Will ERPNext fit our specific business processes?+
That's exactly what the Frappe Framework is for. Custom fields, DocTypes, workflows, scripts, print formats and entire custom apps let us shape the system around your processes — without breaking the upgrade path.
