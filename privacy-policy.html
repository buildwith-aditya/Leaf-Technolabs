Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Introduction

At Leaf Technolabs, we understand that your privacy is important and we take the responsibility of handling your personal and business data seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you interact with our website and services, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over your data.

Information We Collect

We may collect information when you book a demo, contact us for an inquiry, subscribe to updates, or engage us for a project. This generally includes:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project details you choose to share about your business requirements.
  • Technical information automatically collected when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages viewed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your queries, prepare proposals and estimates, deliver and support the services you request, and to keep you informed about offerings relevant to your ERP journey. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function as intended without them.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, hosting, email, or analytics providers), and only to the extent necessary to perform their services. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to comply with our legal obligations, and to resolve disputes. When data is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

Data Protection

We use reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect the data you share with us against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites that we do not operate. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date, and continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
