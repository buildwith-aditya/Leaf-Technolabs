Before you write

Quick answers.

Is the demo really free? What happens in it?+
Yes — completely free and pressure-free. We'll spend 45–60 minutes walking through ERPNext (or Frappe HR / CRM / Helpdesk) using scenarios from your business, answer your questions, and follow up with an honest recommendation and indicative pricing.
We already use ERPNext but it's a mess. Can you help?+
This is one of our favourite engagements. We start with a system audit — configuration, customisations, data quality, performance — then give you a prioritised rescue plan you can execute with us or on your own.
Do you work with clients outside India?+
Yes. ERPNext supports multi-currency, multi-language and country-specific tax setups, and we work comfortably across time zones with remote-first delivery.
What does an implementation roughly cost?+
It depends on modules, customisation and data complexity — but because ERPNext has no licence fees, total cost is typically a fraction of proprietary ERPs. After a short discovery call we'll give you a clear, fixed-scope quote with milestones.
